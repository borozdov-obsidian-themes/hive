# Borozdov Hive

A theme from the Borozdov collection. Two faces — light **Pollen**, a sunlit honey editorial
on white paper, and dark **Smoke**, the same pages by the beekeeper's smoker. Every note
opens on a honey banner, near-black ink carries the rest, soft rounded cards and pill
badges.

![Borozdov Hive in light mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/hive/main/screenshots/light.png)

![Borozdov Hive in dark mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/hive/main/screenshots/dark.png)

## Principles

- **A honey banner.** The title of every note sits on a rounded honey block, in near-black,
  on both faces.
- **Black ink and one yellow.** Near-black ink carries every word, border and button. Honey
  is never a button: it is the banner, a checked task, the highlighter and the warning card,
  with pale pollen for the plain note.
- **Callouts without a rainbow.** Most callouts are mist cards with an ink title; the plain
  note is pollen, a warning turns honey and real trouble is an inverted card.
- **Nothing sharp.** 24px on cards, 16px on buttons and code, pills for tags; the open file
  is a white pill on the mist panel.

## Features

- Light and dark modes, following Settings → Appearance → Base color scheme
- A honey banner behind the title of every note
- Callouts in one yellow and black ink, with an inverted card for trouble
- Tags as mist pills that turn honey under the pointer
- Quiet editing: no focus ring around the note, its title or form fields while you type;
  property names read as labels, not boxed fields
- Text colours meet WCAG contrast on both faces
- The phone layout keeps the same colours and shapes
- No `!important`: every rule can be overridden with a CSS snippet

## Installation

**From the community directory:** Settings → Appearance → Themes → Manage, search for
**Borozdov Hive**, then **Install and use**.

**By hand:** download `manifest.json` and `theme.css` from the [latest
release](https://github.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/hive/releases/latest) into
`<vault>/.obsidian/themes/Borozdov Hive/`, then choose Borozdov Hive under Settings →
Appearance → Themes.

## Font

Alegreya Sans ExtraBold (© 2013 The Alegreya Sans Project Authors) is embedded in
`theme.css` as base64 WOFF2 under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 — see
[`fonts/OFL.txt`](fonts/OFL.txt). One weight, Latin and Cyrillic, for the title and the two
largest headings only.
